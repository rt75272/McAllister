🦋 Moth Camouflage

Peppered moths vary in color. Predators are more likely to spot moths that contrast with tree bark. Choose a habitat and run generations to see how inherited color traits can become more common when they provide camouflage.

What to look for

Variation: The starting population has both light and dark moths.

Selection: Predators catch more moths that stand out against the bark.

Inheritance: Survivors pass their color traits to offspring.

Try switching the habitat after several generations. A color that was well camouflaged may become easier to spot when the background changes.
