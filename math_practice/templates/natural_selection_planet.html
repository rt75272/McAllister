Natural selection, step by step

1
VariationIndividuals in a population have different inherited traits.
2
SelectionIn this habitat, predators catch more beetles that are easier to see.
3
InheritanceSurvivors reproduce, passing shell-color traits to offspring.
4
Population changeAcross generations, helpful traits can become more common.

Individual beetles do not change color to adapt. The population’s trait frequencies shift over generations because some inherited traits lead to more surviving offspring.
