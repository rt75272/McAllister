🍪 Baking Club Recipe #9

Classic Snickerdoodles

Club Date: February 10, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate,   Skill Focus: Creaming butter and sugar, rolling dough, cinnamon sugar, and controlling cookie texture

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are making a soft, buttery cookie with a sweet cinnamon sugar coating, Snickerdoodles!

You have already practiced making cookies. Today we are going to take those skills a step further by learning how creaming butter and sugar affects the texture of a cookie and how cream of tartar gives a classic snickerdoodle its slightly tangy flavor and soft texture.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Creaming Butter and Sugar

One of the most important techniques in cookie baking is creaming butter and sugar.

  • Start with butter that is softened, not melted.
  • Beat the butter and sugar until the mixture looks lighter and fluffy.
  • Scrape down the sides of the bowl when needed.
  • Do not rush this step.
  • Use the correct measuring tools for each ingredient.

Baker Tip: Melted butter and softened butter do not behave the same way. Melted butter can make cookies spread much more than intended.

🍪 Classic Snickerdoodles

Makes: About 24 cookies

Prep Time: About 20 minutes

Bake Time: About 9 to 11 minutes per batch

🛒 The Grocery List

For the Cookie Dough

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 3/4 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

For the Cinnamon Sugar

  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon

🍴 Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Medium mixing bowl
  • Electric mixer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Rubber spatula
  • Cookie scoop or tablespoon
  • Baking sheets
  • Parchment paper
  • Small bowl for cinnamon sugar
  • Cooling rack

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and 1 1/2 cups sugar until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Add vanilla: Mix in the vanilla extract.
  6. Combine: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture. Mix just until a soft dough forms. Do not overmix.
  7. Make the coating: In a small bowl, combine the 1/4 cup sugar and cinnamon.
  8. Scoop the dough: Use a cookie scoop or tablespoon to portion the dough into similar sized balls.
  9. Coat the cookies: Roll each dough ball in the cinnamon sugar until completely coated.
  10. Arrange: Place the dough balls on the prepared baking sheets, leaving several inches between each cookie.
  11. Bake: Bake for approximately 9 to 11 minutes. The cookies should look set around the edges while remaining soft in the center.
  12. Cool: Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a cooling rack.
  13. Enjoy: Once the cookies have cooled enough to handle, enjoy your snickerdoodles!

💡 Baker Tips

  • Softened butter is important. Your butter should be soft enough to mix easily but should not be melted.
  • Cream the butter and sugar well. This helps create the right cookie texture.
  • Do not overmix the dough. Mix just until the ingredients come together.
  • Make your dough balls similar in size. This helps the cookies bake evenly.
  • Give the cookies room. They will spread as they bake.
  • Do not wait for them to look completely firm. Snickerdoodles should stay soft in the center.
  • Use a timer. A minute or two can make a big difference with cookies.
  • Let them finish setting on the pan. Freshly baked cookies are delicate when they first come out of the oven.

🔬 Baking Science, Why Cream of Tartar?

Snickerdoodles are known for their slightly tangy flavor and soft texture. A key ingredient that helps create those characteristics is cream of tartar.

Cream of tartar is an acidic ingredient. In this recipe, it works together with baking soda to help the cookies rise and contributes to the classic snickerdoodle flavor.

Baker Question: What do you think would happen to the cookies if we changed the amount of baking soda or cream of tartar?

🌱 Vegan Option

Snickerdoodles can be adapted for a vegan baker.

  • Replace the butter with a plant based butter designed for baking.
  • Replace the eggs with a suitable baking egg substitute.
  • Check packaged ingredients to make sure they meet your dietary requirements.

Baker Tip: Different egg substitutes behave differently, so follow the package directions for the best result.

🌾 Gluten Free Option

Replace the all purpose flour with a 1 to 1 gluten free baking flour blend designed to replace regular flour.

Check the labels on all packaged ingredients if you are baking for someone with celiac disease or a gluten allergy.

Baker Tip: Gluten free dough can sometimes have a different texture. Give the dough time to come together before deciding whether it needs anything else.

🥜 Nut Allergy Notes

The basic snickerdoodle recipe does not require nuts.

Always check packaged ingredients for allergy warnings and follow classroom allergy safety procedures, especially when sharing baking equipment or ingredients.

✨ Recipe Remix

Once you have mastered the classic snickerdoodle, try creating your own version!

  • Extra Cinnamon: Increase the cinnamon in the coating for a stronger spice flavor.
  • Vanilla Snickerdoodles: Add a little extra vanilla to the dough.
  • Chai Inspired: Add a small amount of ginger, nutmeg, and cardamom to the cinnamon sugar.
  • Orange Spice: Add a little orange zest to the dough for a citrus twist.

Baker Challenge: Design your own cinnamon sugar combination and give your cookie creation a name!

Your goal today is to practice consistency. Cream your butter and sugar carefully, portion your dough evenly, coat every cookie completely, and watch the oven closely.

🧁 Baking Club Recipe #10

Vanilla Cupcakes with Buttercream

Club Date: February 24, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate,   Skill Focus: Cake batter, portioning, testing for doneness, buttercream, and decorating

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are taking our baking skills to the next level with cupcakes and buttercream! We are not just baking today, we are learning how to turn a simple cake into a decorated dessert.

You will practice making a smooth cake batter, dividing it evenly between cupcake liners, checking for doneness, making buttercream, and using a piping bag to decorate your cupcakes.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Portioning Cupcakes

One of the secrets to great cupcakes is making sure they are all approximately the same size.

  • Use a cookie scoop or measuring spoon to portion the batter.
  • Fill cupcake liners consistently.
  • Do not overfill the liners.
  • Try to get the same amount of batter into each cup.
  • Rotate the pan carefully if your oven has hot spots.

Baker Tip: Consistent cupcakes bake at the same rate. If one cupcake is much larger than the others, it may need more time in the oven.

🧁 Vanilla Cupcakes

Makes: 12 cupcakes

Prep Time: About 20 minutes

Bake Time: About 18 to 22 minutes

🛒 The Grocery List

  • 1 1/4 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup whole milk

🍴 Equipment

  • 12 cup muffin pan
  • 12 cupcake liners
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Medium mixing bowl
  • Electric mixer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Rubber spatula
  • Cookie scoop or measuring spoon
  • Cooling rack

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a 12 cup muffin pan with cupcake liners.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Add vanilla: Mix in the vanilla extract.
  6. Combine: Add the dry ingredients in portions, alternating with the milk. Mix just until combined.
  7. Fill the liners: Divide the batter evenly among the cupcake liners. Aim to fill each liner about two thirds full.
  8. Bake: Bake for approximately 18 to 22 minutes, or until the tops spring back lightly when touched and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  9. Cool: Let the cupcakes cool in the pan for several minutes, then transfer them to a cooling rack. Allow them to cool completely before decorating.

🍥 Easy Vanilla Buttercream

🛒 The Grocery List

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons milk or heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt

👩‍🍳 Buttercream Instructions

  1. Beat the butter: Beat the softened butter until smooth and creamy.
  2. Add powdered sugar: Gradually add the powdered sugar while mixing on low speed.
  3. Add flavor: Mix in the vanilla and a pinch of salt.
  4. Adjust the texture: Add milk or cream a little at a time until the buttercream is smooth and spreadable.
  5. Whip it: Beat the buttercream for another minute or two until it becomes light and fluffy.

🎨 Decorating Tip: If your frosting is too stiff, add a tiny amount of milk. If it is too soft, add a little more powdered sugar. Make small adjustments rather than adding a lot at once.

🎨 Beginner Piping Challenge

Today you get to practice using a piping bag!

  1. Place your piping tip inside the piping bag.
  2. Fill the bag about halfway with buttercream.
  3. Twist the top of the bag so the frosting does not come out the back.
  4. Hold the bag directly above the cupcake.
  5. Squeeze with steady pressure.
  6. Move in a small circle to create a swirl.
  7. Stop squeezing before lifting the bag away.

Remember: Your first swirl does not need to be perfect. Piping is a skill that improves with practice!

🔬 Baking Science, Why Do Cupcakes Rise?

Our cupcakes need a little help getting light and fluffy. That help comes from a leavening agent, baking powder.

When the batter heats in the oven, the baking powder produces gas bubbles. Those bubbles expand and help the cupcake rise.

Once the cupcake is baked, the structure of the cake sets and holds its shape.

Baker Question: What do you think would happen if we left the baking powder out of the recipe?

💡 Baker Tips

  • Do not overmix. Once the flour is added, mix just until the batter comes together.
  • Measure carefully. Too much flour can make cupcakes dense and dry.
  • Fill evenly. Consistent portions help your cupcakes bake evenly.
  • Do not open the oven repeatedly. Opening the oven can cause temperature changes.
  • Cool completely before frosting. Warm cupcakes can melt your buttercream.
  • Practice your piping pressure. Consistent pressure creates more even decorations.
  • Have fun with color. Food coloring can turn your buttercream into almost any color.

Baker Tip: Gluten free batters can have a slightly different texture. Follow the flour blend's instructions for the best results.

🍋 Baking Club Recipe #11

Bright and Zesty Lemon Loaf

Club Date: March 10, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate,   Skill Focus: Citrus zest, loaf cakes, careful mixing, and glazing

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are adding a little sunshine to Baking Club with a bright, moist lemon loaf! 🍋

This recipe introduces a new skill, using fresh citrus zest to add lots of flavor. You will also practice working with a loaf pan, checking a cake for doneness, and making a simple lemon glaze.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Using Citrus Zest

The colorful outside layer of a lemon contains natural oils that are packed with flavor. That is called the zest.

  • Wash and dry the lemon before zesting.
  • Use the fine side of a grater or a microplane.
  • Only grate the yellow outer layer.
  • Avoid the white layer underneath, called the pith.
  • The pith can taste bitter.

Baker Tip: Zest the lemon before squeezing it for juice. A whole lemon is much easier to zest than a squeezed lemon!

🍋 Lemon Loaf

Makes: 1 loaf

Prep Time: About 20 minutes

Bake Time: About 50 to 60 minutes

🛒 The Grocery List

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon zest
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

🍋 For the Lemon Glaze

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

🍴 Equipment

  • 9 by 5 inch loaf pan
  • Parchment paper
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Medium mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Rubber spatula
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Microplane or fine grater
  • Citrus juicer
  • Cooling rack
  • Small bowl for glaze

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9 by 5 inch loaf pan and line it with parchment paper, leaving some parchment hanging over the sides for easy removal.
  2. Prepare the dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Combine the sugar and zest: In a large bowl, combine the sugar and lemon zest. Rub them together gently with your fingertips for about 30 seconds. This helps release the lemon oils.
  4. Add the wet ingredients: Add the melted butter, eggs, milk, lemon juice, and vanilla to the sugar mixture. Whisk until combined.
  5. Add the flour: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients. Stir gently with a spatula just until no large streaks of flour remain.
  6. Do not overmix: Stop mixing as soon as the batter comes together. Overmixing can make your loaf tough.
  7. Fill the pan: Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top.
  8. Bake: Bake for approximately 50 to 60 minutes. Begin checking around 50 minutes. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  9. Cool: Allow the loaf to cool in the pan for about 10 to 15 minutes. Carefully lift it out using the parchment paper and transfer it to a cooling rack.
  10. Make the glaze: Whisk together the powdered sugar and lemon juice until smooth. Add the lemon juice gradually until you reach a thick but pourable consistency.
  11. Glaze: Once the loaf has cooled completely, drizzle the glaze over the top.
  12. Slice and enjoy: Let the glaze set for a few minutes before slicing your lemon loaf.

💡 Baker Tips

  • Zest before juicing. It is much easier to zest a whole lemon.
  • Measure your lemon juice. Lemons vary in size, so do not simply assume one lemon equals a specific amount of juice.
  • Do not overmix. Once the flour is added, mix gently and stop when the batter is combined.
  • Check the center. The outside of a loaf can look finished while the center still needs more time.
  • Let it cool before glazing. A warm loaf can cause the glaze to melt and disappear.
  • Make your glaze slowly. Add the lemon juice a little at a time so it does not become too thin.
  • Use parchment paper. It makes removing the loaf much easier.

🔬 Baking Science, Why Does Overmixing Matter?

When flour is mixed with liquid, it develops gluten. Gluten gives baked goods structure, but too much gluten development can make a cake or quick bread tough instead of tender.

That is why bakers often say, "Mix until just combined."

Baker Question: Why do you think we mix the batter less after adding the flour?

This loaf can be adapted for vegan bakers.

  • Replace the butter with plant based butter or a baking appropriate neutral oil.
  • Replace the milk with an unsweetened plant based milk.
  • Replace the eggs with a suitable baking egg substitute.
  • Check the powdered sugar to make sure it meets your dietary requirements.

Baker Tip: Different egg substitutes can change the texture of a loaf, so follow the package directions for the best results.

🌾 Gluten Free Option

Replace the all purpose flour with a 1 to 1 gluten free baking flour blend designed to replace regular flour.

Check the labels on all packaged ingredients if you are baking for someone with celiac disease or a gluten allergy.

Baker Tip: Gluten free batters may benefit from a few extra minutes of resting time before baking, depending on the flour blend you use.

The basic lemon loaf does not require nuts.

Always check packaged ingredients for allergy warnings and follow classroom allergy safety procedures, especially when baking for students with allergies.

🍰 Baking Club Recipe #12

Classic Cheesecake Bars

Club Date: March 24, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced,   Skill Focus: Crumb crust, cream cheese filling, gentle mixing, baking, and chilling

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are making something extra special, cheesecake bars! 🍰

This recipe introduces several new baking skills. You will make a buttery crumb crust, prepare a creamy cheesecake filling, practice mixing without adding too much air, and learn why cooling and chilling are such important parts of cheesecake baking.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Making a Crumb Crust

Our cheesecake bars begin with a simple crumb crust. The goal is to turn crumbs, sugar, and melted butter into a crust that holds together once baked.

  • Crush the cookies or graham crackers into fine crumbs.
  • Mix the crumbs evenly with the sugar.
  • Add melted butter and stir until the crumbs are evenly coated.
  • Press the mixture firmly into the pan.
  • Try to create an even layer from edge to edge.

Baker Tip: Press the crust firmly, especially around the corners. A loose crust can crumble when you cut the bars.

🍰 Classic Cheesecake Bars

Makes: About 16 bars

Prep Time: About 25 minutes

Bake Time: About 30 to 35 minutes

Chill Time: At least 3 hours

🛒 The Grocery List

For the Crust

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted

For the Cheesecake Filling

  • 16 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon all purpose flour
  • Pinch of salt

🍴 Equipment

  • 8 by 8 inch or 9 by 9 inch baking pan
  • Parchment paper
  • Food processor or rolling pin for crushing crumbs
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Electric mixer
  • Rubber spatula
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Mixing spoon
  • Cooling rack
  • Sharp knife

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line your baking pan with parchment paper, leaving some parchment hanging over the sides for easy removal.
  2. Make the crumbs: If needed, crush the graham crackers into fine crumbs.
  3. Make the crust: Mix the graham cracker crumbs and sugar together. Add the melted butter and stir until the crumbs are evenly coated.
  4. Press the crust: Pour the crumb mixture into the prepared pan. Press it firmly and evenly across the bottom.
  5. Bake the crust: Bake the crust for about 8 to 10 minutes. Remove it from the oven and allow it to cool slightly.
  6. Prepare the cream cheese: In a large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
  7. Add the eggs: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed just until combined.
  8. Add the remaining ingredients: Mix in the vanilla, sour cream, flour, and salt until the filling is smooth.
  9. Do not overmix: Once everything is combined, stop mixing. Overmixing can incorporate extra air into the filling.
  10. Pour: Pour the cheesecake mixture over the slightly cooled crust. Smooth the top with a spatula.
  11. Bake: Bake for approximately 30 to 35 minutes. The edges should look set while the center should still have a slight jiggle.
  12. Cool: Remove the cheesecake from the oven and allow it to cool at room temperature.
  13. Chill: Cover the pan and refrigerate for at least 3 hours. Overnight chilling will give an even firmer texture.
  14. Cut: Use the parchment paper to lift the cheesecake from the pan. Cut into bars using a sharp knife.
  15. Serve: Enjoy your cheesecake bars plain or with your favorite topping.

💡 Baker Tips

  • Soften the cream cheese. Room temperature cream cheese creates a much smoother filling.
  • Do not overmix. Mix just until the ingredients are combined.
  • Scrape the bowl. Use a spatula to make sure there are no large pockets of cream cheese hiding along the sides.
  • Do not panic about the jiggle. Cheesecake continues to set as it cools.
  • Be patient. Chilling is part of the recipe, not an optional step.
  • Use a clean knife. Wipe the knife between cuts for cleaner looking bars.
  • Cut after chilling. Cold cheesecake is much easier to slice neatly.

🔬 Baking Science, Why Does Cheesecake Need to Chill?

Cheesecake is different from many of the baked goods we have made because it needs time to set after baking.

As the cheesecake cools, the fats and proteins in the cream cheese mixture firm up. Chilling it in the refrigerator helps the bars become firm enough to cut.

Baker Question: What do you think would happen if we tried to cut the cheesecake while it was still warm?

🌀 Baking Club Recipe #13

Soft and Gooey Cinnamon Rolls

Club Date: April 7, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced,   Skill Focus: Yeast dough, kneading, rising, rolling, shaping, and glazing

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are taking on one of the biggest baking challenges of the year, homemade cinnamon rolls! 🌀

This recipe introduces you to the world of yeast breads. You will learn how yeast helps dough rise, practice kneading, roll dough into a rectangle, spread a filling evenly, cut individual rolls, and make a simple cream cheese glaze.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Working with Yeast

Unlike many of the recipes we have made, cinnamon rolls use yeast to help the dough rise.

  • Yeast needs the right environment to work.
  • Liquid should be warm, not hot.
  • Give the dough enough time to rise.
  • Keep the dough covered while it rests.
  • Be patient, yeast dough takes time.

Baker Tip: If your liquid is too hot, it can damage the yeast. Think warm bath water, not hot bath water.

🌀 Cinnamon Rolls

Makes: About 12 rolls

Prep Time: About 30 minutes

Rise Time: About 1 1/2 to 2 hours total

Bake Time: About 20 to 25 minutes

🛒 The Grocery List

For the Dough

  • 3/4 cup warm whole milk
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 3 cups all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

For the Cinnamon Filling

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt

For the Cream Cheese Glaze

  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons milk

🍴 Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Wooden spoon or rubber spatula
  • Clean work surface
  • Rolling pin
  • 9 by 13 inch baking pan
  • Parchment paper
  • Kitchen towel or plastic wrap
  • Sharp knife or unflavored dental floss for cutting
  • Cooling rack
  • Electric mixer for the glaze

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Warm the milk: Warm the milk until it feels comfortably warm to the touch. It should not be hot.
  2. Activate the yeast: Combine the warm milk, yeast, and a small amount of the sugar in a large bowl. Let it sit for about 5 to 10 minutes. The mixture should become slightly foamy.
  3. Add the wet ingredients: Add the egg, egg yolk, and melted butter. Stir to combine.
  4. Add the flour: Gradually add the flour and salt, stirring until a soft dough forms.
  5. Knead: Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Knead for approximately 5 to 8 minutes, until the dough becomes smoother and elastic.
  6. First rise: Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl. Cover it with a clean towel or plastic wrap and let it rise in a warm place until approximately doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  7. Make the filling: While the dough rises, mix the softened butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt into a spreadable filling.
  8. Roll the dough: Once the dough has risen, gently press out some of the air. Roll it into a rectangle approximately 12 by 18 inches.
  9. Add the filling: Spread the cinnamon filling evenly across the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  10. Roll it up: Starting from one long side, carefully roll the dough into a tight log.
  11. Cut the rolls: Cut the log into approximately 12 equal pieces. Try to make each roll similar in size.
  12. Arrange: Place the rolls into a parchment lined baking pan with a little space between them.
  13. Second rise: Cover the pan and allow the rolls to rise again for approximately 30 to 45 minutes. They should look puffy.
  14. Preheat: Toward the end of the second rise, preheat the oven to 350°F.
  15. Bake: Bake for approximately 20 to 25 minutes, until the rolls are lightly golden and cooked through.
  16. Make the glaze: Beat the softened cream cheese and butter until smooth. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla. Add milk a little at a time until the glaze reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
  17. Cool slightly: Allow the cinnamon rolls to cool for several minutes before adding the glaze.
  18. Glaze and enjoy: Spread the cream cheese glaze over the warm cinnamon rolls and enjoy!

💡 Baker Tips

  • Watch your milk temperature. Very hot liquid can damage yeast.
  • Look for activity. Yeast should begin to foam when activated.
  • Do not add too much flour. The dough should be soft and slightly tacky, not dry and stiff.
  • Use the dough's texture as your guide. Kneaded dough should become smoother and more elastic.
  • Give the dough time. Rising cannot always be rushed.
  • Roll evenly. Try to create a rectangle with a similar thickness throughout.
  • Spread the filling evenly. Leave a small border so the filling does not squeeze out immediately.
  • Cut evenly. Similar sized rolls will bake more evenly.
  • Do not overbake. Cinnamon rolls should be soft and tender, not dry.

🔬 Baking Science, How Does Yeast Work?

Yeast is a living microorganism that helps bread dough rise. When yeast has the right conditions, it uses sugars and produces carbon dioxide gas.

The gas becomes trapped in the dough, creating bubbles. Those bubbles expand as the dough rises, giving our cinnamon rolls their soft, fluffy texture.

🥧 Baking Club Recipe #14

Rustic Fruit Galette

Club Date: April 21, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced,   Skill Focus: Pastry dough, rolling, fruit filling, shaping, and presentation

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are making a dessert that looks fancy but is wonderfully forgiving, a rustic fruit galette! 🥧🍓

A galette is similar to a pie, but instead of placing the dough into a pie dish, we roll it out, add our fruit to the center, and fold the edges over the filling. Today you will practice making pastry dough, keeping it cold, preparing fruit, and creating your own unique design.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Working with Pastry Dough

One of the biggest secrets to good pastry is keeping the butter cold.

  • Use cold butter cut into small pieces.
  • Work quickly so the butter does not become too soft.
  • Do not add too much water.
  • Handle the dough gently.
  • Chill the dough before baking.

Baker Tip: You should still be able to see small pieces of butter throughout the dough. Those pieces help create a flaky pastry.

🥧 Rustic Fruit Galette

Makes: 1 large galette, about 8 servings

Prep Time: About 30 minutes

Chill Time: About 30 minutes

Bake Time: About 35 to 45 minutes

🛒 The Grocery List

For the Pastry

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
  • 4 to 6 tablespoons ice cold water

For the Fruit Filling

  • 3 cups sliced fresh fruit, such as apples, peaches, strawberries, blueberries, or mixed berries
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon, optional
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For Finishing

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 1 tablespoon coarse sugar

🍴 Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Pastry cutter or clean hands
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Rolling pin
  • Sharp knife and cutting board
  • Parchment paper
  • Large baking sheet
  • Small bowl for egg wash
  • Pastry brush
  • Cooling rack

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the pastry: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, and salt.
  2. Add the butter: Add the cold butter pieces. Use a pastry cutter or your fingertips to work the butter into the flour until you have a mixture with some pea sized pieces of butter remaining.
  3. Add water: Add ice cold water one tablespoon at a time, mixing gently until the dough begins to come together. Do not add more water than necessary.
  4. Form the dough: Gently gather the dough into a disk. Do not knead it like bread dough.
  5. Chill: Wrap the dough and refrigerate it for about 30 minutes.
  6. Prepare the fruit: While the dough chills, slice the fruit into similar sized pieces. Place it in a bowl and gently mix with the sugar, lemon juice, cornstarch, cinnamon, and vanilla.
  7. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  8. Roll the dough: Place the chilled dough on a lightly floured piece of parchment paper. Roll it into a rough circle about 12 inches across.
  9. Transfer: Keep the dough on the parchment and place it on a baking sheet.
  10. Add the fruit: Place the fruit in the center of the dough, leaving approximately 2 inches of dough around the outside.
  11. Fold the edges: Carefully fold the dough over the edges of the fruit, working your way around the circle. The center should remain open.
  12. Make the egg wash: Beat the egg with the tablespoon of water.
  13. Brush: Brush the exposed pastry with the egg wash. Sprinkle the crust with coarse sugar.
  14. Bake: Bake for approximately 35 to 45 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and the fruit is bubbling.
  15. Cool: Allow the galette to cool on the baking sheet for at least 15 to 20 minutes before slicing.
  16. Serve: Slice and enjoy your rustic fruit galette!

💡 Baker Tips

  • Keep your butter cold. Cold butter is one of the keys to flaky pastry.
  • Do not overwork the dough. Mix just until it comes together.
  • Use ice water. Cold water helps keep the butter firm.
  • Do not worry about perfection. Galettes are supposed to look rustic!
  • Leave a border. You need enough dough around the fruit to fold over the filling.
  • Do not overload the center. Too much fruit can make the galette soggy.
  • Watch the edges. If the crust browns too quickly, protect the edges with foil.
  • Let it cool. The fruit filling thickens as it cools.

🔬 Baking Science, Why Does Cold Butter Matter?

When cold pieces of butter are baked inside pastry dough, the butter melts and creates tiny pockets between the layers of dough.

Those pockets help create the flaky texture we want in pastry.

Classic Two Layer Cake

Club Date: May 5, 2027,   Skill Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced,   Skill Focus: Layering, frosting, crumb coating, decorating, and presentation

👩‍🍳 Welcome Back, Bakers!

Today we are stepping into the world of cake decorating! 🎂

You have learned many baking skills throughout the year, and today we are putting several of them together. You will bake two cake layers, prepare buttercream, stack and fill your cake, apply a crumb coat, and decorate your finished creation.

⭐ Baker Skill of the Week, Building a Cake

A beautiful layer cake is all about balance and patience.

  • Make sure your cake layers are completely cool before decorating.
  • Try to make your layers as even as possible.
  • Use a small amount of frosting between the layers.
  • Apply a thin crumb coat before the final layer of frosting.
  • Chill the cake when needed to help the frosting set.

Baker Tip: Do not rush the decorating stage. A little patience can make the difference between a messy cake and a bakery style cake.

🎂 Classic Vanilla Layer Cake

Makes: One two layer 8 inch cake

Prep Time: About 30 minutes

Bake Time: About 25 to 30 minutes

Decorating Time: About 30 to 45 minutes

🛒 The Grocery List

For the Cake

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup whole milk

For the Vanilla Buttercream

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 to 4 tablespoons milk or heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt

Optional Decorations

  • Sprinkles
  • Fresh berries
  • Decorative candies
  • Food coloring
  • Edible flowers, if appropriate for your classroom

🍴 Equipment

  • Two 8 inch round cake pans
  • Parchment paper
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Medium mixing bowl
  • Electric mixer
  • Rubber spatula
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Cooling racks
  • Offset spatula
  • Bench scraper, optional
  • Decorating bag and piping tip, optional

👩‍🍳 Step by Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease two 8 inch round cake pans and line the bottoms with parchment paper.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Add the vanilla.
  5. Combine: Add the dry ingredients in portions, alternating with the milk. Begin and end with the dry ingredients. Mix only until combined.
  6. Divide the batter: Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared pans.
  7. Bake: Bake for approximately 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  8. Cool: Allow the cakes to cool in the pans for about 10 minutes. Carefully remove them and place them on cooling racks. The cakes must be completely cool before decorating.
  9. Make the buttercream: Beat the softened butter until smooth. Gradually add the powdered sugar, followed by the vanilla and salt. Add milk or cream a little at a time until the frosting is smooth and spreadable.
  10. Choose your bottom layer: Place one cake layer on your cake board or serving surface.
  11. Add filling: Spread an even layer of buttercream over the top of the first cake layer.
  12. Add the second layer: Carefully place the second cake layer on top. Make sure the cake is centered.
  13. Apply the crumb coat: Spread a thin layer of buttercream over the entire cake. This first layer traps loose crumbs.
  14. Chill: Refrigerate the cake for approximately 15 to 20 minutes to allow the crumb coat to firm up.
  15. Final frosting: Add another layer of buttercream over the cake. Use an offset spatula or bench scraper to smooth the sides.
  16. Decorate: Add sprinkles, piping, berries, or another decoration of your choice.
  17. Admire your work: Step back and look at your finished cake before cutting it!

💡 Baker Tips

  • Cool completely. Warm cake and buttercream do not make a good team.
  • Measure carefully. Even layers begin with evenly divided batter.
  • Do not overmix. Mix the batter only until the ingredients are combined.
  • Use a light hand. Too much pressure can cause crumbs to mix into your frosting.
  • Crumb coat first. This gives you a cleaner final layer.
  • Chill when necessary. A short chill can make decorating much easier.
  • Turn the cake as you frost. Rotating the cake helps create smoother sides.
  • Do not compare your cake to someone else's. Everyone's decorating style is different.

🔬 Baking Science, Why Does Cake Rise?

Baking powder releases gas when it reacts with moisture and heat. Those tiny bubbles expand in the oven and help the cake rise.

When we cream butter and sugar together, we also create tiny air pockets in the mixture. These pockets can help contribute to the cake's light texture.
