✨ Starlight Maze

Collect every crystal and outsmart the roaming drones!

⭐ Score 0 ❤️ Lives 3 💎 Crystals — 🛡️ Shield Off

Press Start, then choose a direction.

Mission controls

Move with the arrow keys or WASD, tap a direction, or swipe on the maze. Your ship keeps moving until it hits a wall or you turn.

How to play

  • Collect every small crystal to win.
  • Large shield crystals let you tag drones briefly for bonus points.
  • A drone hit costs one life; after a hit, choose a direction to continue.

Scores last for this game only and do not award account coins.
